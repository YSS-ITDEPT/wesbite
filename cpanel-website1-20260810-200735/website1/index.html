About AACTS® / Patented platform

Detection intelligence,
built for the field.

Advanced Active Chemical Threat Scanning is the technology platform that turns minute chemical traces into timely, actionable knowledge.

Rather than relying on a single fixed detection profile, AACTS® unites controlled sampling, ion mobility analysis and adaptive detection modes inside one operational workflow.

The result is a system engineered to help security teams move from uncertainty to a defensible decision—rapidly, consistently and at the point of need.

04.1 / Analytical sequence

One trace.
Five analytical stages.

Sampling delivers the trace. AACTS®-AIMS converts that sample into a mobility signature and determines what it may contain.

01

Introduce the trace

Collected vapour, particulate or surface-trace evidence is introduced into the analyser for processing.

SAMPLE
02

Form charged ions

Molecules from the sample are converted into electrically charged particles for mobility analysis.

IONIZE
03

Resolve axial movement

Ions travel along the axial detection path and move at different rates according to their properties.

SEPARATE
04

Process the signal

The resulting spectrum is interpreted, meaningful peaks are identified and background interference is reduced.

INTERPRET
05

Match the signature

The mobility signature is compared with the configured threat library to produce the detection outcome.

IDENTIFY
