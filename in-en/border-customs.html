Operational Scenarios

Border & Customs Applications

Vehicle Checkpoint Screening
HVS rapid vehicle interior sampling without requiring the driver to exit. Wide-area aspiration captures explosive and narcotic vapors from within the cabin and trunk. High-throughput lane processing.
Parcel & Courier Inspection
HVS and HHS screening of parcels, mail, and courier packages for trace explosive and narcotic residues. Rapid non-destructive inspection without opening packages.
Shipping Container Screening
HVS large-volume sampling of shipping containers at sea ports and inland container depots. Detection of narcotics concealed within cargo without full container examination.
Pedestrian Border Crossing
HHS targeted trace detection of pedestrians and their luggage at land border crossings. Combined explosive and narcotics mode. Non-invasive screening without physical contact.
Commercial Freight Inspection
Targeted HHS inspection of commercial freight, palletized goods, and bulk shipments flagged by risk intelligence. Rapid trace confirmation before full physical examination.
Mail Centre Screening
Systematic screening of incoming international mail for explosive devices and narcotics. High-throughput processing with DDMS combined detection mode for maximum threat coverage.
