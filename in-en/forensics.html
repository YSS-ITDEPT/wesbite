Operational Scenarios

Law Enforcement Applications

Post-Blast Investigation
Scene characterization following explosive events using HHS targeted residue collection. Compound identification supports prosecution — identifies explosive type, possible origin, and manufacturing methodology.
Narcotics Lab Identification
Rapid on-site identification of clandestine drug manufacturing operations. Detection of precursor chemicals, intermediates, and finished product compounds. Evidence-grade sample logging.
Crime Scene Profiling
Surface trace analysis of objects, vehicles, currency, and personal effects for narcotic residue. Results in under 20 seconds per sample. Full audit trail for evidence integrity.
Traffic Stop Narcotics
Portable HHS deployment for roadside narcotics screening of vehicles. Rapid detection of cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, and fentanyl residues without field test kits.
Evidence Verification
Confirmation of seized substances as evidence. Identification of compound class and purity indicators. Chain of custody documentation integrated with the AACTS-3000 data management system.
Prison & Correctional
Non-contact screening of visitors, mail, and incoming goods for narcotics at correctional facilities. Deterrence through visible deployment. Detection of fentanyl at trace concentrations.
