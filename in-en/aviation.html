Aviation & Port Application Scenarios

Passenger ETD Screening
Explosive trace detection of departing passengers via HHS hand-swab or targeted air sampling. Combined E+N mode for simultaneous explosive and narcotics screening. Under 20 seconds per passenger.
Checked Baggage
HVS wide-area screening of baggage claim and sorting areas. HHS targeted inspection of individual bags. Integration with existing baggage handling systems for flagged item follow-up.
Air Cargo & Freight
Container and pallet-level screening using HVS for large-volume sampling. Rapid characterization of cargo holds without full unloading. Combined explosive and narcotics detection mode.
Port & Maritime Cargo
Shipping container screening using HVS at port of entry. Vehicle and trailer inspection at port gates. Rapid identification of illicit narcotics shipments without full container unloading.
Aircraft Cabin Screening
Wide-area HVS screening of aircraft cabin and cargo hold between flights. Detection of explosive residues from previous passengers. Supports in-flight security assessments.
Airside Vehicle Screening
Screening of airside service vehicles, catering trucks, and fuel tankers for explosive or narcotics contamination. Rapid checkpoint screening without vehicle disassembly.
Technical Advantage

Why AACTS® for Aviation

High Throughput

Under 20 second analysis cycle per sample. DDMS combined mode eliminates the need for separate explosive and narcotics screening steps — one pass covers both threat categories.

Low False Alarm Rate

TRU-RAD dual-axial IMS with orthogonal ion pathways significantly reduces false positives versus conventional single-axis IMS — critical for minimizing passenger disruption in aviation.

Modular Deployment

Single AACTS-3000 analyzer serves multiple HHS and HVS samplers. Samplers can be repositioned without moving the central analyzer — flexible for dynamic airport checkpoint configurations.
