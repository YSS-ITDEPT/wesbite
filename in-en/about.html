Company Profile

Advanced Detection
Built in India

Anika Sterilis develops and manufactures active chemical intelligence systems for defense, homeland security, healthcare diagnostics, aviation, border control, and industrial safety.

Our AACTS platform is engineered around active sampling, dual-axial IMS analysis, adaptive detection logic, and field-ready workflows that help operators identify trace chemical threats before they become incidents.

Our Mission

Active Chemical Intelligence
for a Safer World

Anika Sterilis was founded with a singular conviction — that the most critical security and health challenges facing the world require detection systems that actively seek threats, rather than passively wait for them. AACTS® is the embodiment of that conviction.

Security Mission

To protect citizens, infrastructure, and national borders through active chemical threat detection that operates where passive systems cannot — at ng–pg sensitivity, in real-time, in any environment.

Health Mission

To democratize clinical diagnostics through non-invasive breath-based disease detection — bringing rapid, reliable disease identification to the point of care without laboratory infrastructure.

National Mission

To build India's sovereign capability in advanced chemical detection technology — developing and manufacturing world-class systems domestically, exported globally, from Indian soil.

Company Journey

Milestones

2017

Company Founded

Anika Sterilis Private Limited established with a mission to develop indigenous advanced chemical threat detection technology for Indian defense and security applications.

2017

AACTS-3000 — First System

First generation AACTS-3000 development completed. TRU-RAD dual axial IMS architecture validated. AERB licensing for Ni-63 ionization obtained.

2018

International Recognition — Astor Platinum Award

AACTS-3000 wins Best CBRNE Detection System at the prestigious Astor Platinum Awards, USA. International validation of AACTS® detection performance.

2022

AMTZ Facility — 123,842 Sq.Ft

Full-scale manufacturing facility operational at AMTZ Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. ISO 13485-aligned quality systems. End-to-end production capability from R&D through final system assembly.

2022

Healthcare Breath Analyzer — TB Validation

AACTS® breath analyzer achieves clinically validated performance for tuberculosis screening: 95.7% sensitivity, 91.3% specificity, ROC AUC 0.935 — a world-first application of IMS to non-invasive TB diagnostics.

2022

ISBS Excellence Award & House of Commons Recognition

ISBS Healthcare Technology of the Year 2022 for breath analyzer application. Recognized at the House of Commons, UK, for innovation in healthcare diagnostics technology.

2024

AACTS® Ecosystem Rebrand

AACTS-3000 established as part of the comprehensive AACTS® ecosystem naming framework. Platform expanded to cover 30+ detection domains.

2026

Global Deployment Scale

AACTS® systems deployed across defense, aviation, healthcare, and industrial domains globally. Active engagement with sovereign governments on national-scale deployment programs.
