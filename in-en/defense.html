Operational Scenarios

Defense Application Areas

AACTS® is configured for six primary defense and military application categories — each leveraging the same underlying TRU-RAD X-AIMS detection physics with domain-optimized DDMS settings.

Military Base Security
Perimeter and access point screening for personnel, vehicles, and supplies entering secure military installations. HVS wide-area deployment with HHS targeted follow-up. Explosives and CWA combined mode.
Ordnance & Ammo Depot Safety
Continuous scheduled sampling of storage facilities for explosive vapor signatures. Early detection of degradation, improper storage, or tampering. Prevents catastrophic incidents at ammunition depots.
CBRNE Response
Rapid field deployment for chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, and explosive incident response. Combined mode detection. Identification of threat agent for immediate protective action.
Vehicle & Convoy Screening
HVS rapid vehicle interior sampling for IED components, explosives, and contraband. Checkpoint deployment without requiring vehicle disassembly. High-throughput screening capability.
Post-Blast Forensics
Scene characterization following explosive events. HHS targeted residue collection for evidence-grade compound identification. Chain of custody data management. Supports prosecution and intelligence efforts.
Intelligence & Surveillance
Covert sampling capability for intelligence operations. Identification of improvised explosive device (IED) components and precursor materials. Supports force protection and counter-IED operations.
